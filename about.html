About Smart Calculator

Smart Calculator started from a simple frustration: needing a quick BMI check, a rough loan estimate, or a GPA calculation and having to dig through cluttered websites full of pop-ups and unrelated ads just to find a basic number box. We built this site to be the opposite of that — open it, enter your numbers, get your answer.

There's no account to create, nothing you type is saved anywhere, and every calculation runs right in your browser. Whether you're checking in on your health, planning a loan repayment, or working out your semester GPA, the goal is the same: a clear answer in a few seconds.

What you'll find here

BMI Calculator

Height, weight, age and sex-aware BMI with a visual gauge and category breakdown.

Loan Calculator

Estimate monthly repayments, total interest, and total cost on any fixed-rate loan.

GPA Calculator

Add subjects with grades and credits to get a credit-weighted GPA instantly.

Our approach

We keep things simple on purpose. No logins, no dashboards, no tracking your calculation history. Each tool includes plain-language explanations of the formula behind it, so you're not just getting a number — you understand where it came from.

A note on accuracy

These calculators give estimates based on standard formulas (BMI, amortized loan payments, credit-weighted GPA). They're useful for quick planning and general screening, but they don't replace professional medical advice, a lender's official quote, or your institution's official grade records.
