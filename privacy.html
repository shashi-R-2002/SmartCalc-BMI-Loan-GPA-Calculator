Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Smart Calculator ("we", "our", "this site") provides free BMI, loan, and GPA calculators. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the site.

Data you enter into the calculators

Every calculation on this site — BMI, loan, and GPA — runs entirely in your own browser using JavaScript. The height, weight, age, income, or grade details you type in are never sent to, or stored on, our servers. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you entered.

Cookies and analytics

We may use standard analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand overall site traffic — for example, how many people visit and which pages are popular. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Advertising

This site may display advertisements through Google AdSense or similar ad networks to keep the calculators free to use. These networks may use cookies to show relevant ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also manage cookie preferences through your browser settings at any time.

Third-party links

Any outbound links on this site (for example, to sources or references) lead to third-party websites with their own privacy practices. We're not responsible for the content or privacy policies of those sites.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in how the site works or in applicable law. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us at hello.smartcalculator@gmail.com.
